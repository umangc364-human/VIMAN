FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does VIMAN detect the wall?

VIMAN uses a pretrained AI image-segmentation model that can identify objects and surfaces such as walls in a room photograph.

Can I undo my design?

Yes. Use the Reset button to return to the original uploaded image.

Do I need a professional photograph?

No. A normal phone photograph can be used. A clear image with the wall visible gives the AI more information to work with.

Is VIMAN just an AI image generator?

No. VIMAN is designed around identifying the actual wall in your uploaded photograph and changing that area rather than generating an unrelated room.
